Privacy Policy

Files are stored temporarily under unguessable job IDs, never listed publicly, and auto-deleted after the retention period (default 1 hour). No account, no tracking of audio contents. Processing requires CPU on our server; if an external inference provider is configured, audio is transmitted to that provider under its terms — see MODEL.md. You may delete a job anytime to remove its files immediately.
